The non-Pro DX4600 has its own Celeron N5105 model entry instead of inheriting the DX4600 Pro's specifications. Upstream CLI LED control was confirmed on DSM by [sbbbsbbb](https://github.com/sbbbsbbb); this plugin and the physical bay order remain unconfirmed. Other DX4600 variants stay recognized with unknown specifications; the plain model and those variants show front and rear only until their inside layout is established.
